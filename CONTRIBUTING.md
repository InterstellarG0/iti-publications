# ITI Public Foundations — Inquiries and Contributions

ITI welcomes questions, source recommendations, factual corrections, and constructive criticism about its published research topics. Send inquiries to admin@interstellargaming.co. Please reference the topic ID and identify the source or claim involved.

At this stage, ITI is **not accepting unsolicited code, confidential documents, unpublished inventions, or technical submissions**. Sending an inquiry does not create employment, compensation, partnership, endorsement, or an obligation to respond. Do not send personal records, credentials, sensitive operational details, or materials you lack permission to share.

The CC BY-NC 4.0 license applies only to material specifically designated as covered by that license. It does not license the ITI name or marks, private materials, or third-party content. Commercial reuse requests must receive separate written permission.

ITI may preserve documented corrections and material disagreements in a transparent revision history. An issue, email, or suggestion is not automatically an approved research conclusion.
