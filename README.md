# ITI Research Library — Public Foundations v0.2

**Organization:** Interstellar Technologies Initiative, LLC  
**Date:** 2026-11-11

## Purpose
Provide a public entry point into ITI's research questions, organizational foundations, and editorial methodology. This publication is informational: it does not assert scientific validation, product readiness, or partnerships.

## What the public will see
- ITI's general mission: explore decentralized, portable, educational and resilient technology systems
- A catalog of 98 candidate **research topics** (ITI-RP-001–098), not completed papers
- A transparent research workflow and publication-status vocabulary
- A contribution and inquiry policy

## Published notes
These preliminary notes publish specific methods and questions, not validated findings.

- [Operational Discernment: Questions, Resources, and Reassessment](research/2026-11-11-operational-discernment.md)
- [Core Loop Tuning: From Player Friction to Traceable Changes](research/2026-11-11-core-loop-tuning.md)

See [release history](CHANGELOG.md). The 98-topic candidate inventory remains distinct from these published notes.

## What remains private
Unpublished manuscripts, private notes, internal handoffs, AI transcripts, logs, test data, budgets, operational plans, sensitive designs, and personal information are not included. The public topic index is not a publication of underlying working drafts.

## Research method
Capture → Classify → Investigate → Outline → Draft → Critically Review → Verify → Publish → Revise.

Preserve the criticism. Convert it into a question. Follow the evidence.

## Reader guidance
Catalog entries are proposed research directions, not validated findings, engineering specifications, investment offerings, or claims that products exist. Topic status is inherited from the source inventory and may not reflect work in other private project areas.
