# Guest Welcome: A Thirty-Second Orientation

Date: November 13, 2026
Status: Proposed checklist v0.1; rehearsal required.
Origin: Founder requested a brief, adaptable welcome; ChatGPT organized the choices and routine.

## Before sessions
- Review available targets and the experience that has actually been tested.
- Review each booking's preference if supplied.
- Have simple controls and equipment-handling instructions ready.

## One optional booking question
**What would you most enjoy tonight?**
- A shared date-night experience.
- Learning how space photography works.
- Gaming while we explore.
- A relaxed sky session.
- Help me choose.

Guests choose an interest, not a personality label. Do not infer ability, diagnosis, or preferences from age, relationship status, or appearance. Collect only what is needed to deliver the session.

## Arrival
- Confirm the choice, or ask the question if it was unanswered.
- Ask: **Want to take the controls, or would you rather I guide you?**
- Explain the tested flow and the available takeaway, including weather limitations.
- Demonstrate safe handling before participation.
- Let guests change their emphasis as they go.

The thirty-second aim applies to the welcome, not to all training or accessibility support.

## Session emphasis
| Choice | Starting approach |
|---|---|
| Shared date night | Shared target choice and capture |
| Learn photography | Explain framing, light collection, and image development |
| Gaming | A tested game during capture, with progress check-ins |
| Relaxed sky session | Comfortable pacing and optional questions |
| Help me choose | A simple guided start, adjusted through feedback |

Gaming remains optional. A tablet may support questions; acknowledge uncertainty and verify consequential or disputed answers. Several models agreeing is not independent verification.

## Optional keepsake
A small logo in a corner or separate border, with accurate target and date information, can be added before printing. Test legibility, crop margins, time, and cost. A portable backdrop for an optional guest photo is another proposed feature; test stability and obtain permission before the business shares any guest image. Photos are not a condition of participation.

## Afterward
Record one improvement, with minimal personal data. Do not treat a completed checklist as proof that a guest enjoyed the session.
