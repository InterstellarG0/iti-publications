# Human-AI Delegation: The Bottleneck Was Listing, Not Selling

**Date:** November 18, 2026
**Status:** Working research note; lessons drawn from live shop operations, not a completed study
**Origin:** Founder-directed lesson development with AI-assisted drafting. This public adaptation excludes personal records, supplier details, costs, and internal operating records.
**Connected catalog topic:** ITI-RP-110, Daily Human-AI Collaboration (candidate)

## The lesson
Most sellers never start because listing dozens of products by hand is the work nobody wants to do. The bottleneck was listing, not selling.

## The split
Man decides, AI does the repetitive work. The human sets the rules, approves each product and price, and checks the result. The AI does the repetitive listing work inside those rules. After a few listings done together and verified, the AI picks the pattern up.

## The rules that make it safe
- Verify everything. A listing is not done until the price, cost, stock, and compliance answers have been checked against the real source.
- Cost before price. Never set a price before the real cost is known, and never price below cost.
- If it cannot clear a profit floor and stay competitive, skip it. Not entering a trade is also trading.
- Small batches, human approval before anything goes live. The human still decides, still verifies, and still carries the result.

## What it is not
This is not a claim that delegation removes the work. The gain is that the repetitive part stops being the reason nothing starts.
