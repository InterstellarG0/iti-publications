# Downsizing: Compare the Whole Cost

**Author:** Anthony P., founder, Interstellar Technologies Initiative, LLC (ITI)  
**Date:** November 13, 2026  
**Status:** Field-manual planning exercise v0.1; untested

## Question
Would a smaller or more mobile living arrangement improve affordability and daily functioning? Removing one bill is only part of this question. Compare the same services over the same period, with upfront cash shown separately from recurring costs.

## Worksheet
| Category | Current arrangement | Proposed arrangement |
|---|---|---|
| Housing | Rent and included services | Site or housing charge, deposits, fees |
| Transport | Rental or ownership costs | Vehicle purchase/finance, insurance, fuel, repairs |
| Utilities | Included and separately billed | Electricity, water, waste, heating/cooling, connectivity |
| Setup | Existing equipment | Acquisition, inspection, installation, moving |
| Reliability | Backup arrangements | Repair and relocation reserves, service interruption |
| Daily needs | Work, accessibility, household care | Whether those same needs are met |

Record quoted amounts, assumptions and unknowns separately. Do not label an assumption a saving. Account for the timing of purchase costs and describe both monthly cash flow and longer-term ownership costs without counting the same acquisition twice.

## Constraints are part of the design
Pets, accessibility, dependable utilities, work access and a suitable permitted location belong in the requirements. Verify a particular site's written stay limits, full-time occupancy terms, utility charges and pet rules; do not generalize one park's terms to all parks. A temporary stay arrangement does not by itself settle legal residence.

Portable connectivity is another testable component. Assess a permitted usable installation, power and real performance before removing an existing connection. Keep the equipment project and a housing move as separate decisions so one experiment does not force the other.

## Exercise
List the current total, obtain specific alternative quotes, identify an assumption that could reverse the result, and choose the smallest feasible test. Record a reason to proceed, revise or stop. No savings, prices, legal conclusions or recommended purchase are established by this exercise.
