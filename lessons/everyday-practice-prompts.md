# ITI Everyday Lessons: Practice Prompts

November 2026. Educational drafts; learning outcomes unevaluated.

Each prompt uses fictional information. Practice on paper or with an adult facilitator when an AI service is unsuitable or unavailable. Follow the actual service’s minimum age. Learners can pass.

## Calm boundaries

```text
Help me practice a calm boundary. In a made-up game chat, someone keeps joking about me after I ask them to stop. Give me one short reply and an exit. Keep it respectful. Explain when to ask a trusted adult for help.
```

Check: Did the response use the supplied facts, respect the limits and leave room for your own choice? Identify one assumption before using it.

## Friendships and peer pressure

```text
In a made-up group chat, friends pressure me to share a private screenshot. Give me two short ways to say no and one different activity to suggest. Use no insults or real names. Let me choose my own wording.
```

Check: Did the response use the supplied facts, respect the limits and leave room for your own choice? Identify one assumption before using it.

## Privacy and screenshots (outline only)

```text
Use an invented example to help me decide whether to share a screenshot. Ask who needs it and what private details it contains. Give me a safer option. Do not ask me to upload a real message.
```

## Attention and taking breaks

```text
I opened a feed for a game tip and kept scrolling. Help me practice Notice, Choose, Switch. Give me one question to ask myself and two next steps I can choose from. Include taking a break. Avoid guilt or a rigid schedule.
```

## Gossip, help and AI

```text
In a fictional class, I saw Sam look toward another desk. Someone says Sam cheated. Separate what I saw from guesses. Give two possible explanations and one fair question. Do not decide that Sam is guilty.
```

## Reconciliation

```text
My fictional build needs 10 iron. My inventory says 7. Compare the goal and record. Show what is collected, still needed or uncertain. Give one next step. Use only these numbers and do not invent completed work.
```

## GitHub beyond code

```text
Help me plan a project library without coding. My fictional project is learning to draw. Suggest a README and three note files. Explain how to save a change in a browser. Use invented information. Mark drafts clearly and do not publish anything.
```

## Better Input. Better Output.

```text
Help me plan a 20-minute drawing practice. I have paper and a pencil. Give me four short steps and one way to check my progress. Ask if an essential detail is missing. Do not assume I own other supplies.
```

Check: Did the response use the supplied facts, respect the limits and leave room for your own choice? Identify one assumption before using it.

## Reusable prompt frame

```text
Goal: [what I want to practice]
Context: [fictional or safe relevant details]
Limits: [what to avoid or respect]
Format: [what the answer should look like]
Check: [how we will tell whether it fits]
If an essential fact is missing, ask rather than invent it.
```

* AI can be wrong. Check important answers. Keep personal information private.

A clearer prompt helps communicate intent but does not guarantee correct output. Test the lesson with a new fictional example, not a real private conversation.
