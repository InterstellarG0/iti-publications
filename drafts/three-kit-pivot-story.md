# Protect the Mission by Creating Options
Date: November 13, 2026
Status: Founder-authorized public concept draft v0.1; no operating results or demand validation.
Origin: Founder discussion; AI-assisted synthesis. This is a selected public adaptation, not a transcript release.

## The pivot
Kit 001 began around portable astronomy/astrophotography with gaming. Outdoor observing introduces conditions that an indoor station does not require. The proposed response is to develop two complementary indoor concepts alongside it.

| Kit | Proposed experience | Relationship |
|---|---|---|
| 001 | Astronomy/astrophotography with gaming | Original direction remains; outdoor imaging requires suitable conditions |
| 002 | Laptop-centered rendering and creative workstation | Explore an indoor creation experience |
| 003 | Gaming PC and streaming peripherals | Explore an indoor gaming and content-creation experience |

“Protect 001” means testing whether additional useful services can eventually contribute to sustaining the original mission. It does not mean that more spending reduces risk by itself. New kits add capital needs, setup work, maintenance and scheduling dependencies.

## What simultaneous means at this stage
Draft the concepts and explain them together. Cost, fund and test each actual build before offering it. Equipment can be shared only when scheduling allows; three concepts do not equal three independently deployable stations. Multiple concurrent sessions would require adequate dedicated equipment and supervision.

## What the public can help answer
What specific activity would someone want to do? What would they need to leave with for the session to be useful? Which parts of the description are unclear? These are invitations to voluntary feedback, not reservations or fundraising offers.
No prices, paid bookings, suppliers, venues, build deadlines or profitability are established.

## A constructive “swarm” introduction
“Swarm” describes an openly ITI-authored series of related posts and demonstrations across platforms. The purpose is to encourage curiosity, practical learning, creative agency and constructive feedback. Use clear authorship and accurate concept labels.
It does not describe covert persuasion, fake supporters, fabricated testimonials, coordinated harassment or claims to repair people's minds. No psychological or clinical effect is established.
People may question the idea or decline it. Record specific feedback separately from reach, likes and sales.

## Sequence
Define each experience → verify and fund the complete shopping list → assemble and test → approach existing venues → run bounded pilots → measure attendance, repeat interest, costs and partner feedback → revise or expand.
A proposed trailer-based shared creative space remains a later option; no land or trailer commitment accompanies this release.
