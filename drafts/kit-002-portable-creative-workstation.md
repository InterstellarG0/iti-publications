# Kit 002 — Portable Creative Workstation
Date: November 13, 2026
Status: Public concept draft v0.1. Not assembled, benchmarked, priced, or available for booking.
Origin: Founder-defined concept; AI-assisted organization and critical review.

## Purpose
Explore an indoor, laptop-centered station for rendering, editing, and creative projects. The laptop, peripherals, and additional monitors are the founder's proposed core. Specific components, applications, performance targets, funding, and operating arrangements remain unresolved.

## Relationship to Kits 001 and 003
Kit 001 retains its astronomy/astrophotography and gaming direction. Kit 002 explores indoor creative work; Kit 003 explores indoor gaming and streaming. Developing both drafts alongside 001 creates options. It does not establish three working kits or authorize buying all three at once.
Indoor sessions may reduce dependence on observing weather. Revenue supporting Kit 001 is a business hypothesis, not a demonstrated protection or guarantee.

## Proposed modular inventory
| Module | Draft scope | Unresolved |
|---|---|---|
| Compute | One laptop intended for demanding creative work | CPU, GPU, memory, storage, cooling and application requirements |
| Displays | Expandable external-monitor setup | Number, size, ports, dock compatibility and actual transport fit |
| Input | Keyboard, mouse and headset | Accessibility and application needs |
| Connections | Suitable dock/adapters and labeled cables | Display support and power delivery under load |
| Transport | Protective cases and secured accessories | Packed dimensions, weight, setup and solo handling |
| Session | Project materials and save/export method | Software permissions, user data cleanup and participant deliverable |

A laptop advertised as powerful is not a measured rendering service. Select a representative project first; size the hardware to its requirements.
Sharing equipment across kits can reduce duplication, but one shared device cannot be promised to two simultaneous bookings.

## Example session to rehearse
Use an ITI-owned or appropriately licensed sample project. Unpack, connect a display, open the project, make one guided change, render/export the result, save a participant copy if requested, remove session data, and repack. Record failures rather than hiding them.
This is a proposed test, not a claim that a session has occurred.

## Proposed acceptance record
Record setup/reset/packing times; sustained workload behavior; actual render time; display compatibility; energy use; transport fit; successful export; and whether a new participant understands the instructions. Set the intended session duration and acceptable performance before buying hardware or promising an offer.
Keep the first session attended. Youth use, staffing, accessibility, software permissions and venue suitability must be resolved for the actual activity.

## Next decisions
Define one initial application and project; select the minimum complete bill of materials; obtain current delivered quotes; secure affordable funding; assemble and rehearse; then approach suitable existing venues with tested equipment.
No site, partner, staffing level, booking price or launch date is established.

## Visual identity — proposed
Theme: **The Workshop**. Graphite, cool cyan and restrained violet. A compact laptop, portable monitors and organized project materials. If used, the golden ant retains the adult, slightly broader build, practical hoodie and headset. Images must say CONCEPT and show transportable equipment.
Public invitation: “What would you want to create at a portable workstation?”
