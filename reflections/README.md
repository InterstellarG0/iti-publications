# Reflections: The Subfolders of Life

A growing collection of short founder reflections, with questions and actions readers can adapt to their own projects. The first collection draws from selected November 11–15 material. It is not an exhaustive review of the archive.

These are the founder’s perspectives and educational adaptations, with AI-assisted editing. They are not quotations from Marcus Aurelius or claims of philosophical equivalence. The connection is the practice of reflecting on daily conduct.

## Make your own version

See [the starter guide](starter-guide.md). Choose folders that fit your life. Keep source credit and your own additions distinguishable. Only include information you are comfortable publishing; a folder named private inside a public repo is still public.
