# The Subfolders of Life: Starter Guide

A GitHub project library, no coding required.

Begin with one project and one note. Add folders when they help you find something. You can practice with local files or paper. Eligible GitHub users can follow [the browser lesson](../lessons/github-life-library-and-prompting.md).

| Folder or file | What belongs here |
|---|---|
| README.md | Purpose, folder index and current next step |
| reflections/ | Reviewed observations, questions and lessons |
| projects/ | Goals, attempts, deliverables and evidence |
| skills/ | Skills practiced and examples of using them |
| decisions/ | Decisions, reasons and later corrections |
| sources/ | Sources, attribution, permissions and adaptations |
| handoffs/ | Completed, pending, blocked and uncertain work |
| your-own-folder/ | A category that serves your actual life |

## Keep separate private and public records

Keep raw personal records in a separate private location. Prepare a reviewed public derivative. Do not put passwords, medical details, addresses or other people’s private conversations into the starter. A private-named subfolder cannot change a public repository’s visibility. Deletion does not necessarily remove old versions or copies.

## Reflection template

```text
Title:
Date or period:
Observation: What actually happened?
Interpretation: What do I think it means?
Uncertainty: What could I be missing?
Decision or lesson:
One action to try:
Result: Pending until checked.
Sources and credit:
My additions or changes:
Public sharing review: What did I omit or anonymize?
```

## Copyable AI handoff

```text
Help me organize these reviewed, nonsensitive notes into my project library.
My goal: [goal]
Current folders: [list]
New category I want: [category or none]

Preserve my original separately from your interpretation.
Suggest where each note belongs and why. Use existing folders when they fit.
Keep dates, sources, decisions and disagreements traceable.
Distinguish observations, hypotheses, decisions and verified results.
Draft a public reflection with one open question and one small action.
Mark missing facts instead of inventing details, achievements or earnings.
Credit source ideas and distinguish my additions. Paraphrasing alone is not
proof of originality or permission to reuse material.
Do not publish, delete or change access from this prompt alone.
Return proposed paths, draft text, unresolved questions and a change summary.

Reviewed notes: [fictional or safe material]
```

AI can be wrong. Check important answers. Keep personal information private. No AI account is required for this workflow. Follow each service’s minimum age.

## Weekly reconciliation

Compare your plan with the actual files and completed work. Correct stale statuses. Keep useful disagreements. Record what changed and choose a next step. Keep a separate backup and check that you can open it.

## Reuse and credit

Adapt the folder categories to your needs. Reuse of ITI’s written examples follows its existing CC BY-NC notice; commercial reuse requires separate permission. Third-party sources keep their own terms. Do not label ITI writing as entirely your original work or imply ITI endorses your project.
