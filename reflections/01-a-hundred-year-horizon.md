# A Hundred-Year Horizon

Reflection period: November 15, 2026
Public adaptation prepared November 2026.
Author perspective: Anthony P., Interstellar Technologies Initiative, LLC. AI-assisted editing.

## Reflection

I want to contribute to a future where people can cooperate through open tools while retaining independence. A hundred-year horizon asks what later generations could inspect, correct and carry forward. The useful contribution begins with a small task I can actually finish.

## Open question

How can a long vision guide today’s work without becoming a substitute for it?

## One action

Finish one useful record, label its limits and make it portable.

## Source and limits

Founder conversation, November 15, 2026, with the closing vision preserved in the private field-manual handoff. This is an aspiration, not a forecast.
