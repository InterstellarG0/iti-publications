# Recognition and Room to Build

Reflection period: November 15, 2026
Public adaptation prepared November 2026.
Author perspective: Anthony P., Interstellar Technologies Initiative, LLC. AI-assisted editing.

## Reflection

I admire leaders who give people room to create and then help their work reach an audience. My appreciation for Elon Musk reflects that leadership ideal. The engineering credit also belongs to the people doing the work. Recognition may encourage contribution alongside fair compensation and clear terms.

## Open question

Am I crediting the actual contributors as well as the organization or leader?

## One action

Name a specific contribution, link the work and explain what it helped you do.
